## Existing-trace audit (complete, no new games)

Source: recency run update1 collection, generated by the original champion: **200 training fights, 6,024 searched decisions / 6,719 total states**. Scored collecting-model priors on stored feature rows and aligned all logged child visits to the full encoded legal menu. Worker logs only visited children; absent children have zero visits. Every reconstructed policy matches encoded normalized visits; all searched roots total2,000 simulations.

Fight-balanced descriptive estimates (normal approximate95% intervals across the200 fight means; conditional on one fixed model, not independent decision samples):
- At least one legal action gets <=2 visits: **40.5% of decisions [38.8,42.1]%**.
- Fraction of legal actions getting <=2 visits: **10.5% [10.0,10.9]%**.
- At least one completely unvisited action: **2.64% [2.28,3.00]%**.
- Total network-prior probability on <=2-visit actions: **0.60% [0.57,0.63]%** on average.
- Search overturns network argmax: **26.7% [25.6,27.8]%**; **15.9% [14.9,17.0]%** after excluding identical-token switches (representational grouping, not physical-equivalence proof).
- At least one action with prior>=.05 gets <=10 visits: **4.69% [4.08,5.30]%** of decisions.
- Root chosen action receives **63.0% [62.2,63.9]%** of visits on average.
- All observed menus have2–11 legal actions; m16 covers every menu in this sample.

**Interpretation:** there is room to change root coverage, but no obvious proof of widespread harmful starvation. Almost all severely neglected actions already have tiny network priors; search already overturns priors substantially. Thus confidence in a large win-rate gain should be modest, and a bounded falsification test is preferable to a sweep. Recorded Q means lack sampling variances and cannot establish that neglected actions are better.

Machine-readable results: run `out/{results.json,root-states.parquet}`; script `root_allocation_audit.py`; log `logs/audit.log`. An initial read-only assertion failed because visited-only child logging was not accounted for; corrected to explicit zero-visit alignment and verified against encoded policy targets. No gameplay retries/data regeneration.
