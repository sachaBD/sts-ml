## What is being trained

- **Trunk:** `inventory_v1` (`dim=192`, `layers=4`, `heads=6`)
- **Existing heads:**
  - `value = "outcome"`: 20-bin combat margin distribution (10 enemy-HP loss bins + 10 player-HP win bins)
  - `auxiliary = true` (training-only, weight `0.25`): `hp_lost`, `enemy_hp_lost` (policy batches) and `turns_remaining` (combat-balanced `value_batch = 128`)
- **New head (`ThinkingHead`):**
  - `nn.Linear(192, 4)` read out from context token `hidden[:, 0]` (`thinking_rounds = 4`, sample format `5`)
  - **Loss:** MSE over finite round targets (objective weight `1.0` on policy-side batches; gradients flow into shared trunk)
  - **Export:** ONNX emits `['value', 'logits', 'thinking']`; C++ `infra::Prediction` / `search::Evaluation` do not consume `thinking` yet

## Collection & supervision setup

- **Main self-play (`20,000` Act 2 boss fights / iter):**
  - Pure prior sampling: `sims = 0`, `gumbel_scale = 1.0` (no main-line search policy improvement; isolates cheap trajectory generation + probe collection on a strong warm-started prior)
  - Uncertainty-weighted deck sampling (`concentration = 0.5`, `history_iterations = 15`)
- **Side-branch probe:**
  - Triggered uniformly with `p = 1/16` (`0.0625`) on every player decision (including cheap roots, which mask policy/value targets and supervise only `ThinkingHead`)
  - Runs deterministic evaluation-mode Gumbel (`simulations = 256`, `considered_actions = 16`, `particles = 16`, `gumbel_scale = 0`, `spend_even_budget = true`) on an independent RNG stream (`RandomStream::thinking_probe`)
  - Main fight never plays the probe's move and never shares `Answers` cache with it
- **Checkpoint targets (`rounds[0..3]`):**
  - Emitted at the end of each completed sequential-halving width (up to 4 rounds when `>= 16` legal actions; fewer rounds when `< 16` actions, trailing slots `NaN`-masked)
  - `prior` = `argmax(root.logits)`; `selected` = current Gumbel halving leader (ties broken by legal action order)
  - Target = `0.0` if `selected == prior`; else `selected_q - prior_q` from the probe's backed-up root edge Qs at that checkpoint (`NaN` if either edge is unvisited)

## What we are testing & caveats to check when checkpoints land

1. **Signal per compute-hour:** Does zero-grading-cost in-tree `selected_q - prior_q` teach the context token to separate high-gain roots from `0`-gain roots on unseen decks?
2. **Trunk interference:** Does `weight = 1.0` MSE into `hidden[:, 0]` + `sims = 0` self-play preserve or degrade 256-sim Gumbel win rates on `validation_{champ,collector,automaton}` and `legacy_{core,extended}` (evaluated every 2 iterations)?
3. **Known label biases to audit before setting play thresholds:**
   - *Selection optimism (winner's curse):* `selected` maximizes over noisy 16-world Qs on the same worlds used to score `selected_q - prior_q`, biasing mean targets upward where `selected != prior`.
   - *Frozen `prior_q` after early halving elimination:* if `prior` is cut in Round 1, later rounds still subtract its noisy low-visit Round-1 `prior_q` from a high-visit `selected_q`.
   - *Context-only readout (`hidden[:, 0]`):* action tokens attend into context through the 4 encoder layers, though `hidden[:, 0]` does not explicitly receive final policy logits.
