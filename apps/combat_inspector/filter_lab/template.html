How it works (glossary)
VOI (Value of Information)
The expected win-rate gain of our final chosen root move if we expand a candidate node or sample another chance world. Computed in closed form from how much an observation could move the belief (innovation SD) and how large a move is needed to flip the root leader, with shared transpositions cancelled via reach weights. Stops as soon as best VOI ≤ price λ.
Abs win % (V / Q)
Every node's backed-up absolute win probability: exact 0% / 100% if terminal, otherwise max over its actions. Unexpanded actions start from a policy-informed Kalman prior anchored to the node's greedy Turn+1 rollout (μ₀ = anchor + 0.03 · Δlogit), preserving cumulative policy gaps along deterministic root prefixes.
Thinking head (th)
At every evaluated node, the NN predicts win-rate gain at 8 / 24 / 64 / 160 evals. Its maximum (shown as th on every node) sets the exact Gaussian epistemic prior SD √(2π)·max(thinking), floored at 0.5 pp · √(v(1−v)) and capped by distance to 0% / 100%.
Turn-boundary rollouts & CRN
Each newly created in-turn node rolls out greedily to the Turn+1 boundary so moves are compared on the same horizon. Root-prefix chance edges share common random worlds W1, W2, … paired against the policy-prior line's control variate β_w = V_prior(W_w) − V_root, cancelling draw luck from W1 onward.
Kalman uncertainty
Chance edges run a scalar information-form Kalman filter across sampled worlds. Empirical scatter updates measurement noise, posterior mean, variance, and innovation SD; unpaired deep chance edges floor observation noise by Bernoulli draw variance 0.01·v(1−v).
No turn or depth limit
End turn is just a chance edge whose children are Turn+1 decision nodes. Expanding them naturally crosses into Turn+1, Turn+2, … whenever VOI beats price λ.
