How it works (glossary)
Prior move
The policy's top move. Every root move's score is its estimated win-chance gap to the prior, in percentage points (pp). The prior starts at 0 and only moves if follow-up credit applies to it. ★ marks the current leader.
Policy %
softmax of the policy logits: what the network would play with no search.
Starting scores
Before any evidence: (policy logit − prior's logit) ÷ 30.
Opening
A thinking head forecasts the gain from searching with 8 / 24 / 64 / 160 evals. If the best gain per eval beats the price, that budget becomes the reserve. Otherwise play the prior immediately.
Reserve
Until it's spent, the search buys the best purchase even below the price (the bar in the timeline marks where it runs out). After that, a purchase must beat 0.05 pp per eval.
World W
One sampled guess at hidden information (draw order etc.), up to 16. Comparisons are paired: both moves play on the same world, so luck cancels.
Line
A root move, then the policy's top choice at each decision until the next turn starts. The leaf is scored by the value head (0% or 100% if the fight ended). Each new position visited costs one eval, and cached positions are free.
Root comparison
Play move B and the prior on the same world. The difference is one observation of B's gap.
Follow-up test
Replay a saved line but take the runner-up at the first decision after the root move. If the runner-up looks better, root moves that reach that position get credit in proportion to how often they reach it.
Belief update
A scalar Kalman-style filter blends the starting score with the observed differences. A purchase is priced by how much the next observation could still move the belief, and so change which move leads.
