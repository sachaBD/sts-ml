Champ viewer

Ironclad A20 vs The Champ. Load a held-out bench deck, edit the board, and compare the PV network (instant value and priors) with the teacher (guided-rollout MCTS, 20k sims, 8 particles) and its playouts. Local only: needs gui/champ_server.py.

PV value / priors come from a network trained on our agent's play and share its blind spots. Playout win rates are teacher fights from this public state (hidden draw order resampled per playout).
